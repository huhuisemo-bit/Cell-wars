# Cell Wars Roadmap

## MVP

- Stable Pygame launch.
- One player vs simple AI.
- ATP victory.
- Basic card play and reaction execution.

## Alpha

- More races and race powers.
- More reactions and structures.
- Better AI priorities.
- Educational explanations for each reaction.

## Beta

- Full Hot Seat support for 1-4 players.
- Card art, animations, and sound.
- Save/load and settings.
- Balance pass and playtesting notes.
